Why Texture Matching Matters

Here's what separates a good drywall job from a bad one: anyone can fill a hole, but making the repair vanish takes texture work. We identify your wall's finish — orange peel, knockdown, skip trowel, or smooth — then build the patch in layers: tape and mud feathered wide so there's no hump, texture sprayed or troweled to match the surrounding pattern, and paint blended with the right sheen. Flat paint on a patch next to eggshell walls will flash every time; we match that too.

Our Paint Process (No Shortcuts)

  • Prep first: furniture and floors protected, holes patched, seams re-taped where needed, glossy spots sanded, gaps caulked.
  • Prime right: stain-blocking primer over water marks and patches — paint alone doesn't hide stains, it just tints them.
  • Two coats: proper coverage and even sheen, with clean cut lines at every edge.
  • Florida-grade exteriors: pressure wash, scrape failing paint to solid, caulk penetrations, spot-prime bare wood, then quality exterior acrylic.

Drywall & Painting FAQ

Do I need a licensed contractor in Florida for drywall and painting?

No — drywall repair, patching, texture matching, and interior/exterior painting are squarely in handyman scope in Florida and don't require a specialty license. If water damage involves structural framing rot or mold beyond a contained patch, we'll flag it and advise on next steps before proceeding.

How much do drywall repair and painting cost in Clearwater, FL?

Most of our jobs fall between $95 and $450: small patches and touch-ups run $95–$250, while multi-patch repairs or full-room repaints run $250–$450. Whole-home or exterior work gets a custom written quote — call (727) 479-5969 for a free estimate.

Can you match my wall texture?

Yes — texture matching is our specialty. We replicate your wall's orange peel or knockdown pattern, density, and sheen so the patch blends invisibly instead of flashing a smooth spot on a textured wall.

Can you repair drywall damaged by a water leak?

Yes. We cut out the damaged section back to solid drywall, treat against mold, patch, texture-match, prime with stain-blocking primer, and repaint. We also fix the plumbing source, so one crew handles the whole job.

Do you paint exteriors in Florida's climate?

Yes — with proper prep and products: pressure wash, scrape and caulk failing areas, prime bare spots, and quality exterior acrylics rated for UV and moisture. Prep is what makes an exterior paint job survive Florida.
