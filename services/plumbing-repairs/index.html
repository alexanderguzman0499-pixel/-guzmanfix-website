Older Pinellas Plumbing: What to Know

If your home still has galvanized pipes, you're living on borrowed time — internal corrosion restricts flow, discolors water, and eventually springs leaks at the threads. We can replace accessible corroded sections, swap failing valves and fixtures, and track down the cause of low pressure at a single fixture versus the whole house.

What we won't do: sell you a patch when you need a repipe. If your system is failing house-wide, we'll tell you straight that it's licensed-plumber territory and help you understand what to ask for when you call one.

Handyman Scope vs. Licensed Plumber — Honest Answer

  • Our sweet spot: fixture replacements, leak repairs on accessible lines, valves, disposals, dishwasher installs, and toilet work.
  • Needs a licensed plumbing contractor: whole-house repiping, sewer line repair or replacement, new plumbing rough-in for additions, and anything requiring a plumbing permit with the city or county.
  • Our promise: if your job crosses that line, we say so on the spot — no upsell, no guesswork. We'd rather point you to the right trade than do the wrong job.

Plumbing Repairs FAQ

Do I need a licensed plumber in Florida, or can a handyman do plumbing repairs?

For fixture-level work — faucet and toilet replacements, supply-line repairs, shut-off valves, garbage disposals, and dishwasher installs — a skilled handyman is the right, cost-effective call. Whole-house repiping, sewer work, or new rough-in requires a licensed plumbing contractor in Florida, and we'll tell you straight if your job falls in that category.

How much do plumbing repairs cost in Clearwater, FL?

Most of our plumbing jobs fall between $95 and $450: small repairs like valves and flappers run $95–$250, while toilet replacements and dishwasher installs run $250–$450. Larger work gets a custom written quote — call (727) 479-5969 for a free estimate.

My home has old galvanized pipes. Can you still help?

Yes — we work with galvanized systems regularly: replacing accessible corroded sections, swapping failing valves and fixtures, and diagnosing pressure issues. If the whole system needs repiping, that's licensed-plumber work and we'll say so upfront.

Do you install dishwashers and garbage disposals?

Yes — dishwasher installation is one of our most-requested jobs: full swap with water, drain, and electrical hookup, leveling, securing, and a test cycle. Same thorough approach for garbage disposal replacements.

What should I do if I have an active water leak?

Shut off the nearest valve — or your home's main shut-off — then call us at (727) 479-5969. We prioritize active leaks and repair the resulting drywall damage too, so one crew handles everything.
