Tampa Bay Homes: What We Clean For

Humidity is the quiet enemy: it grows mildew in shower grout, under sink cabinets, and along window tracks faster than most homeowners expect. Add tracked-in beach sand, spring pollen coating every lanai, and hard-water spots on glass shower doors, and a "quick wipe-down" never quite cuts it. Our checklists are built around these local patterns — grout, tracks, fans, and baseboards get attention on every visit, not just on deep cleans.

What's the difference between a standard cleaning and a deep cleaning?

A standard cleaning maintains your home: dusting, vacuuming, mopping, bathrooms, kitchen surfaces, mirrors, and trash. A deep cleaning resets it: baseboards, blinds, grout lines, inside the oven and fridge, ceiling fans, window sills and tracks, and light fixtures. Most homes benefit from a deep clean once or twice a year, with standard cleanings in between.
