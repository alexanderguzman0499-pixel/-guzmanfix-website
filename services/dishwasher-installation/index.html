What a Proper Install Includes

  1. Shut off water and power — verified off before anything is touched.
  2. Disconnect and remove the old unit — water, drain, and electrical separated cleanly; old unit hauled away.
  3. Inspect the supply valve and line — if the valve is frozen or the line is corroded, we flag it and replace it with your approval.
  4. Connect water, drain, and electrical — new braided supply line, drain routed with proper high loop or air gap, electrical wired and secured.
  5. Level and secure — unit leveled front-to-back and side-to-side, anchored to the countertop or cabinet sides so it never walks.
  6. Test cycle — a full cycle run while we watch for leaks at every connection and confirm proper fill, wash, and drain.

How long does a dishwasher installation take?

About 2–3 hours for a straightforward swap, including the test cycle. If we find a corroded valve or a drain connection that needs correcting, we fix it on the spot with your approval — that can add 30–60 minutes.

My dishwasher isn't draining or cleaning well. Repair or replace?

Units under 8–10 years old with clogged filters, kinked hoses, or a bad drain pump are usually worth repairing. If the tub is cracked or the control board is dead on a 12+ year-old unit, replacement is smarter money. We diagnose honestly and tell you which side you're on.

Do you handle the water, drain, and electrical hookups?

Yes — all three. Hot water supply (replaced if worn), drain tied into the disposal or sink drain with proper high-loop or air-gap routing, and the electrical connection wired safely. Then leveled, secured, and test-cycled before we leave.
