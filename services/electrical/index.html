Safety & Code Notes

  • Breaker off, tester on: we verify every circuit is dead before touching a wire — no assumptions, no exceptions.
  • GFCI where water lives: kitchens, bathrooms, laundry, garage, and exterior outlets get proper ground-fault protection.
  • Aluminum wiring handled right: CO/ALR devices or approved pigtail connectors with antioxidant compound — never copper-only devices on aluminum wire.
  • Fan-rated support: ceiling fans get boxes and bracing rated for the load, not repurposed light boxes.

Handyman Scope vs. Licensed Electrician — Honest Answer

  • Our sweet spot: replacing and installing devices on existing circuits — fans, fixtures, outlets, switches, dimmers — plus troubleshooting.
  • Needs a licensed electrician: panel upgrades, new circuits run from the panel, service entrance work, whole-house rewires, and anything requiring an electrical permit.
  • Our promise: electricity deserves respect. If your job needs a licensed electrician, we tell you on the spot and help you describe the problem clearly when you call one.

Electrical FAQ

Do I need a licensed electrician in Florida, or can a handyman do electrical work?

Device-level work — replacing ceiling fans, light fixtures, outlets, switches, and dimmers on existing circuits, plus troubleshooting — is standard handyman scope. Panel upgrades, new circuits from the panel, and permitted work need a licensed electrician in Florida, and we'll tell you immediately if your job crosses that line.

How much does electrical work cost in Clearwater, FL?

Most of our electrical jobs fall between $95 and $450: single device swaps run $95–$250, while ceiling fan installs or multi-fixture updates run $250–$450. Larger work gets a custom written quote — call (727) 479-5969 for a free estimate.

Can you install a ceiling fan where a light fixture is now?

Usually yes — but the existing box must be fan-rated for the weight and vibration. We check the box first and install a proper fan-rated brace box when needed, rather than hanging a fan from a light-only box.

My breakers keep tripping. Can you find the problem?

Yes — we troubleshoot tripping breakers, dead outlets, and flickering lights methodically, isolating the circuit and checking devices and connections. If the fault is in the panel or needs new circuits, we'll refer you to a licensed electrician.

Do you work safely around older wiring?

Absolutely. We kill power at the breaker, verify dead with a tester before touching anything, use proper CO/ALR-rated connectors for aluminum wiring, and never cut corners on grounding.
